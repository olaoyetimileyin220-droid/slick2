How our forecasts work

◈
Trend & volatility

We fit the recent 60-day price trend, shrink it toward zero because momentum rarely lasts, and size the range from the last 30 days of volatility.

⚡
Technical signals

RSI, MACD and moving averages are calculated from daily candles and combined into a single Buy / Neutral / Sell reading.

⛨
Honest ranges

Every projection comes with a likely range, not a promise. Real prices regularly land outside it, especially around news.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

Prices and candles come from public exchange and market-data feeds. Market cap and trending data come from CoinGecko, sentiment from the Alternative.me Fear & Greed Index, and headlines from public crypto news feeds.

How accurate are the projections?

They are statistical estimates, not predictions. The base line continues a dampened version of the recent trend, and the shaded band shows where price landed about 68% of the time given recent volatility. Treat them as context, not advice.

What does the technical summary mean?

Each indicator votes Buy, Neutral or Sell. The meter shows the balance of votes. A Buy reading means most indicators lean bullish right now. It says nothing about the future.
